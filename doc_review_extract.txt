0064 [body] BACKGROUND OF STARTUP
0065 [body] Startup Summary
0066 [body] Pormatch is our AI-assisted outfit planning website designed to help users choose coordinated clothing combinations. It considers the user’s activity, preferred style, size, fit, and color choices when providing recommendations. The platform aims to provide three matching-color recommendations and three complete outfit combinations, giving users a manageable set of options to compare.
0067 [body] Our MVP shows the main outfit-planning process through the landing page, outfit generator, outfit results, and saved outfits. Users can enter their preferences, review suggested looks, and save their chosen outfits for future use. We also plan to support individual clothing-piece changes and outfit scheduling to make the planning process more flexible.
0068 [body] Background of the Problem
0069 [body] Choosing what to wear can take time when a person is unsure which clothing pieces and colors work together. Aside from appearance, users may consider their destination, activity, weather, comfort, and personal style. Having several clothing options does not always make the decision easier because users still need to identify which combinations suit their needs.
0070 [body] Based on the answers of our three college student respondents, outfit selection is a difficulty they experience at different frequencies. Respondent 1 experiences it almost every day, Respondent 2 almost every time she goes somewhere, and Respondent 3 sometimes. They usually spend around 10 to 20 minutes choosing an outfit, although some occasions take longer. All three have also changed clothing because the colors or pieces did not match.
0071 [body] Their current approaches include searching for inspiration online, asking friends for suggestions, trying different combinations, and choosing simple or neutral clothing. These methods can help, but users still need to decide whether an outfit suits their own preferences and activities. Through Pormatch, we aim to bring these decisions into one guided process that presents coordinated options and short explanations.
0072 [body] Purpose and Objectives
0073 [body] Our goal is to make outfit planning easier and help users feel more confident about their clothing choices. We want to provide useful recommendations while allowing users to make the final decision based on their preferences.
0074 [body] Specifically, we aim to:
0075 [body] • Provide three outfit suggestions based on the user’s selected activity and clothing preferences.• Help users coordinate clothing pieces and colors through matching recommendations.• Allow users to compare suggested outfits and choose a suitable combination.• Support individual clothing-piece changes so users can adjust a look before saving it.• Allow users to save preferred outfits and support advance planning through a proposed scheduling feature.• Use likes and dislikes to improve future recommendations.• Gather user feedback to evaluate the usefulness and ease of use of the platform.
0076 [body] Initial Scope
0077 [body] Our initial product focuses on outfit recommendations and saved looks using a clothing illustration database. The MVP includes a public landing page, a generator that accepts activity, style, and color preferences, a results page displaying three outfit options, and a saved outfits page.
0078 [body] Customization, scheduling, and feedback-based improvements remain part of our planned development. Our initial testing will focus on college students because they are the group represented in our interviews. We will also consider weather and comfort when improving the recommendations, since respondents identified these as important factors.
0093 [body] PITCH DECK
0094 [body] Problem Description
0095 [body] Our interviews show that some college students find it difficult to choose outfits that look coordinated and suit their activities. They spend time checking colors, trying clothing combinations, and replacing pieces before leaving. Respondent 2 also described feeling frustrated when an outfit does not look as expected and takes extra time to change.
0096 [body] Online fashion content and advice from friends provide inspiration, but users still need to apply these ideas to their own preferences. Our project addresses this problem by offering three coordinated outfit options in one place. We aim to make comparison and selection easier, while further testing will determine whether Pormatch actually improves the outfit-planning experience.
0097 [body] Target Market Segment
0098 [body] Our initial target market consists of college students who want assistance with everyday outfit choices. This group is our first priority because our three respondents are college students who shared difficulties with outfit selection and color coordination.
0099 [body] High school students, young adults, professionals, and people preparing for events are also potential users. However, we still need to gather feedback from these groups to understand their specific needs. Fashion and lifestyle businesses may become promotional partners through advertisements or featured promotions, although this opportunity also requires further validation.
0100 [body] Solution
0101 [body] Pormatch provides a guided process for choosing coordinated outfits. A user selects an activity or destination, preferred style, and main color. Profile information, such as size and preferred fit, helps personalize the recommendations. The platform then presents three complete outfit options that users can review and compare.
0102 [body] The results include coordinated clothing pieces, footwear, and accessories, together with matching information and short explanations. Users can save a preferred look and review it later instead of starting the selection process again. We also plan to allow individual-piece changes and scheduling to give users more control over their outfit plans.
0103 [body] Through this process, we aim to address the uncertainty described by our respondents. Prototype testing will help us determine whether the recommendations are suitable and whether users find the website helpful enough to use again.

0110 [body] • Likes and DislikesA planned feedback feature will allow users to express their preferences and help guide future recommendations.
0111 [body] • Outfit Sharing and Customer AssistanceThese proposed features will allow users to share selected looks and request help when using the website.

0191 [body] Validation Board
0193 [body] Explanation and Discussion
0194 [body] Our validation board shows how we checked the outfit-planning problem and how we plan to test Pormatch. We gathered answers from three college students, labeled Respondents 1, 2, and 3. All three experience difficulty choosing outfits and have changed clothing because the colors or pieces did not match. Respondent 1 usually spends 10 to 15 minutes choosing an outfit, Respondent 2 spends 15 to 20 minutes, and Respondent 3 spends around 10 minutes. Sometimes, they take longer. Their answers helped us identify color coordination, weather, comfort, activity, and personal style as important considerations.
0195 [body] Based on these findings, we will continue testing whether Pormatch can help students choose suitable outfits. Our riskiest assumption is that users will find our recommendations useful enough to choose a look and return to the website. We plan to test the prototype with 10 students, with a target of at least 8 choosing or customizing a suitable outfit without help. We also plan to check whether at least 6 of 10 students save an outfit and return to use it within one week. These tests are still pending. The interviews support the problem, while the next tests will help us evaluate our solution.
